Data source: SMARD’s monthly congestion-management series

Before analysing curtailment, it’s worth being precise about what this data actually measures -- its scope, its frequency, and how its numbers are calculated. This notebook covers SMARD’s monthly series; the next chapter covers a second, higher-frequency source, and the comparison chapter reconciles the two.

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import pandas as pd

from pecdr.paths import ProjPaths
from pecdr.smard_redispatch import download_smard_chart_series

paths = ProjPaths()
paths.ensure_directories()

redispatch = pd.read_parquet(paths.redispatch_by_source_file)
RENEWABLE_SOURCES = ["Wind_Onshore", "Wind_Offshore", "Photovoltaik"]
colors = plt.rcParams["axes.prop_cycle"].by_key()["color"]

Attributes of this data source¶

AttributeValue
Grid levelTSO and DSO combined -- checked directly: SMARD separately publishes a TSO-vs-DSO split for renewables (s-nepm_re_eb-1-1x), and summing its two columns lands within 0.3% of this series’ renewable total for the same period. Not TSO-only.
FrequencyMonthly
Measure types includedNot redispatch-only. Summing this series’ reduction and increase columns across all technologies matches, almost to the GWh, the sum of SMARD’s three separately-published measure-type totals (Countertrading + Grid reserve + Redispatch-with-market-power-plants) -- see the verification section below. Countertrading and grid-reserve volume are folded into this “by energy source” view, not excluded from it.
MethodologyEx-post, counterfactual. The compensable quantity (“Ausfallarbeit”) is defined as theoretical feed-in minus actual feed-in, computed after the measure using either a frozen pre-measure baseline or (for wind) actual wind speed plus the turbine’s certified power curve -- see BDEW’s methodology guide. This is not a live instructed-power reading.
Location dataNone. Purely a national total -- no TSO, region, or plant-level breakdown in this series.

Verifying our understanding against SMARD’s own page¶

SMARD publishes a yearly breakdown by measure type (s-nepm_entw-1-1) that isn’t the series we use for the technology charts below, but reproducing its numbers is a good independent check that the CSV-parsing approach (pecdr/smard_redispatch.py) is sound. See SMARD: “Volume of measures stable in the year as a whole” for the interactive chart this reproduces.

Relevant facts about this data¶

Countertrading has no meaningful technology attribution. It’s a market transaction (buying/selling power to relieve a constraint), not an instruction to a specific named plant, so it can’t be attributed a technology -- it should land in Unbekannt. Checked directly: monthly Countertrading volume (s-nepm_ct-1-1) correlates with Unbekannt (summed across both directions) at 0.95 over the whole series, though Unbekannt runs 1.4-1.8x larger than Countertrading alone -- the rest is plausibly grid-reserve or “not attributable” redispatch, which also lack a specific plant to attribute.

countertrading = download_smard_chart_series("s-nepm_ct-1-1", ["countertrading_gwh"])
unbekannt = redispatch.loc[redispatch["energy_source"] == "Unbekannt"].groupby("month")["gwh"].sum().rename("unbekannt_gwh")
ct_vs_unbekannt = countertrading.set_index("date").join(unbekannt, how="inner")
print(f"correlation(countertrading, unbekannt): {ct_vs_unbekannt['countertrading_gwh'].corr(ct_vs_unbekannt['unbekannt_gwh']):.2f}")
correlation(countertrading, unbekannt): 0.95

Feed-in management is essentially zero in recent years (see the 2024 row above) -- the older, EEG-based curtailment mechanism has been folded into Redispatch 2.0’s unified framework rather than continuing as a separate category.

Takeaways¶

  • This is a TSO+DSO, monthly, ex-post counterfactual figure that folds in countertrading and grid-reserve volume -- not a narrow, redispatch-only, TSO-only, real-time reading. Keep that in mind when comparing it against any other source (see the comparison chapter).

  • Wind dominates curtailment volume; offshore is curtailed disproportionately to its fleet size; PV’s smaller curtailment tracks its own generation season.

  • Unbekannt -- mostly countertrading, which has no plant to attribute a technology to -- is the single largest category, bigger than any one generation technology.
